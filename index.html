Waheeb Yaqub, PhD

Human-Computer Interaction Researcher
School of Computer Science · The University of Sydney

I study how people judge and interact with information in digital environments. My research focuses on information credibility, misinformation, scientific information integrity, social media, and human-centred technologies.

Research

Information Credibility

Understanding how people assess the credibility of online information and the cues that shape those judgements.

Scientific Information Integrity

Investigating how retractions, corrections and changes in scientific evidence are communicated across digital platforms.

Misinformation & Social Media

Studying how misinformation spreads and how interface design can support more informed decisions.

Human-AI Interaction

Exploring credibility, transparency and information evaluation in AI-mediated environments.

Selected Projects

Scientific Retractions & Social Media

Understanding how people evaluate social-media news based on scientific research that has subsequently been retracted.

Retraction Visibility at Web Scale

Investigating whether retraction information remains visible as scientific findings move across publishers, news and online platforms.

Retract Trace

Developing human-centred approaches for communicating changes in the status of scientific evidence.

Credibility Assessment Methods

Studying how research methods such as think-aloud studies and crowdsourcing influence observed credibility judgements.
