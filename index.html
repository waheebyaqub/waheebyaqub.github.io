Human-Computer Interaction Researcher
School of Computer Science · The University of Sydney

I study how people judge and interact with information in digital environments. My research focuses on information credibility, misinformation, scientific information integrity, social media, human-AI interaction, and how AI is reshaping education and work.

Research

Information Credibility

Understanding how people assess the credibility of online information and the cues that shape those judgements.

Scientific Information Integrity

Investigating how retractions, corrections, and changes in scientific evidence are communicated across digital platforms.

Misinformation & Social Media

Studying how misinformation spreads and how interface design can support more informed decisions.

Human-AI Interaction

Exploring credibility, transparency, and information evaluation in AI-mediated environments.

AI, Engineering Education & Work

Examining how engineering curricula and entry-level recruitment are adapting to GenAI, human-AI collaboration, and changing workforce capability requirements.

Selected Projects

Scientific Retractions & Social Media

Understanding how people evaluate social-media news based on scientific research that has subsequently been retracted, and investigating how interface cues can communicate changes in scientific status.

Retraction Visibility at Web Scale

Investigating whether retraction information remains visible as scientific findings move across publishers, scholarly databases, news, social media, and other online environments.

Retract Trace

Developing human-centred approaches for tracing scientific claims and communicating changes in the status of the underlying evidence.

Credibility Assessment Methods

Studying how research methods such as think-aloud studies and crowdsourcing influence observed credibility behaviours and judgements.

Comment Summarisation & Credibility

Exploring how summaries and representations of social commentary around online claims influence people's understanding and perceptions of news credibility.

AI Readiness in Engineering Education & Work

Analysing Australian engineering curricula and entry-level job advertisements to understand how GenAI capabilities are being reflected in education, graduate recruitment, and future workforce expectations.
